## 💼 About Me

I currently work at **Banco do Brasil (DITEC)** as a **Fullstack Developer**, contributing to strategic technology projects.  
My role involves building robust and scalable solutions that integrate back-end, front-end, and data processing in cloud environments.

I hold a **Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems** and have solid experience with **Java using Quarkus**, **Angular**, and **Python with Spark**.  
I also work with multiple database technologies, including **OracleSQL**, **DB2**, **MongoDB**, **Hive**, and **Impala**.  
My focus is on developing high-performance, reliable systems while applying best practices in architecture, security, and data management.

I’m passionate about technology, continuous learning, and applying software engineering principles to solve complex enterprise challenges.
